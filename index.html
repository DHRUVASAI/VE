How We Work

Our 5-Step Manufacturing Process

1

Fabric Sourcing & Testing

Premium raw materials undergo GSM audit, color fastness testing, and tear strength analysis before selection.

2

Custom Pattern Drafting

Ergonomic patterns designed to your exact specifications — fit, style, and comfort for every body type.

3

Precision Cutting & Embroidery

Computer-aided cutting with high-density logo embroidery, screen printing, and custom branding.

4

Industrial Stitching & Assembly

Reinforced double-needle stitching on industrial machines for maximum durability and professional finish.

5

Quality Audit & Packaging

Every unit passes a multi-point quality inspection before custom bulk packaging and dispatch.
